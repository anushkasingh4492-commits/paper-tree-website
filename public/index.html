Made for teaching

Everything you need to
make assessment simpler.

Keep test preparation organised and give learners a clear, focused testing experience.

✎

Test creation and question banks

Teachers can select questions and build papers. Full paper generation is available on Silver and Gold plans; Gold also includes adding institute-created questions.

◎

Branded student experience

Present the student platform with your institute's identity. Gold includes a branded Android app and shareable APK, as described in the product brochure.

◷

Timed online tests

Students attempt scheduled assessments online. Tests submit automatically when the timer expires, according to the brochure.

▤

Results and progress reports

Review marks, subject-wise graphs, batch ranks and PDF reports. Question-level time reports are included on Silver and Gold.

✦

Practice and AI guidance

Students can practise by subject, chapter and difficulty. AI performance summaries are available on Silver and Gold; AI weak-area tests are included on Gold.

♧

Institute and parent workflows

Manage student access and upload student lists using Excel. Gold includes parent reports through WhatsApp; availability may depend on setup.
